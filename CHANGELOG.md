# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-11-17

- Published five case templates for claims, evidence, acceptance, corrections, and policy decisions.
- Added a standard-library validator for required files, table structure, unique IDs, claim/run references, coverage, verdict statuses, reviewer/author separation, dates, and required evidence fields. It does not execute recorded commands or determine whether evidence is true, sufficient, safe, or independently reviewed.
- Added two synthetic examples: notification delivery and observation snapshots.
- Added a 21-test local suite.
